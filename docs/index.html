            Limits

            What this data cannot show

            • Athletic aid only. The federal file reports athletically related aid. It does not show academic or other institutional aid that athletes also receive, so it cannot measure the total discount to athletes.
            • Self-reported, with judgment calls. Colleges differ in what they count as athletic aid, how they split shared costs among teams, and how they count coaches. Small differences between colleges are not meaningful.
            • No revenue comparison. Most small colleges report athletics revenue equal to expense because the institution covers the difference. Revenue is left out.
            • Coeducational colleges only. The law does not require single-sex colleges to file, and the service academies and a few colleges that take no federal aid do not appear.
            • Conference membership is for 2024-25 and comes from outside the federal file. A college that has changed conferences since then is shown with its 2024-25 conference.
